Doraemon and his friends are quite popular! Doraemon himself is a robotic cat from the 22nd century who travels back in time to help a young boy named Nobita. Some of the main characters include: Doraemon: A blue robotic cat who has a magical pocket from which he can pull out various futuristic gadgets to help Nobita. Nobita Nobi: The main protagonist, a young boy who often gets into trouble but is always helped by Doraemon's gadgets. Shizuka Minamoto: Nobita's kind and intelligent classmate who is often shown as the voice of reason among the group. Takeshi "Gian" Goda: A strong and tough boy who sometimes bullies Nobita but is also a loyal friend. Suneo Honekawa: A wealthy boy who likes to show off and often manipulates others, especially Nobita. These characters, along with Doraemon, form the core group around whom many adventures and life lessons are centered in the series.............
